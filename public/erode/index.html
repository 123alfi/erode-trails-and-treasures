A little planning, a lot of exploring

Your Erode travel guide

Thoughtful essentials for a memorable, responsible visit.

When to visit

November to February offers generally cooler weather. Winter is rewarding for birdwatching at Vellode. Water levels and access at dams vary by season.

Getting around

Erode Junction is well connected by rail. Local buses serve major towns; a taxi is useful for rural destinations. Coimbatore is the nearest major airport.

Travel gently

Carry water, leave no litter, and keep your distance from wildlife. Forest visits need authorised access. Never enter dam waters during releases.

A taste of Kongu

Try local Kongu Nadu cooking and browse Bhavani’s colourful handwoven jamakkalam carpets. Ask before photographing people or entering weaving workshops.
